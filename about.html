Dean's Welcome

A Tradition of Engineering Rigor and Discovery

"Engineering is not merely the application of formulas; it is the audacity to envision a world that does not yet exist and build it with precision, empathy, and environmental stewardship."

Established in 1974, Engineering College has grown from a regional polytechnic institute into an internationally recognized engineering powerhouse. Our students do not simply study technology in textbooks; they construct orbital cubesats, design zero-emission urban microgrids, program surgical robots, and architect resilient cyber infrastructures.

With over 45 dedicated research laboratories, an average student-faculty ratio of 14:1, and an active network of 12,500+ alumni leading Fortune 500 tech firms, our college is a launching pad for students passionate about solving the world's most pressing challenges.

Our Guiding North Star

Mission, Vision & Core Principles

The principles that govern our educational methodology, research ethics, and community leadership.

Our Mission

To educate visionary, technically proficient, and socially conscious engineers through immersive laboratory practice, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and high-impact scientific inquiry.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as an elite center of engineering discovery where sustainable solutions to energy, computational intelligence, climate resilience, and robotics are invented and deployed.

Our Values

Integrity, empirical rigor, inclusivity in STEM, environmental responsibility, and an unrelenting curiosity to challenge conventional engineering paradigms.

Our Journey

Five Decades of Engineering Innovation

From our humble founding to our present standing as an internationally acclaimed technical college.

1974

Founding of the College

Established with inaugural departments in Mechanical and Civil Engineering, graduating its first cohort of 42 engineers.

1989

ABET Accreditation

Received formal ABET accreditation across all undergraduate degree programs, cementing international standard conformity.

2012

Innovation Hub Opening

Inaugurated the 120,000 sq.ft. Advanced Robotics & Clean Energy Center funded by public-private aerospace partnerships.

Today • 2026

AI & Quantum Expansion

Housing 45+ research labs, cutting-edge GPU supercomputing clusters, and a student body representing 48 nationalities.

State-of-the-Art Spaces

World-Class Research Facilities

Students gain direct hands-on access to specialized engineering equipment from year one.

Nanofabrication

Class-100 Cleanroom & Micro-Fab

Equipped with photolithography, electron beam evaporation, and atomic layer deposition for silicon and semiconductor research.

Autonomous Systems

Autonomous Robotics Arena

Features Vicon motion-capture cameras, omnidirectional ground robots, UAV flight cages, and real-time ROS2 edge testbeds.

Aerospace Testing

Subsonic & Supersonic Wind Tunnel

High-speed aerodynamic test facility with particle image velocimetry (PIV) and computerized load-cell balance measurements.

Academic Accreditation

Accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET

Our undergraduate degree programs in Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Civil Engineering are accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET, http://www.abet.org. This certifies that our curricula meet the rigorous standards established by the global engineering profession.
