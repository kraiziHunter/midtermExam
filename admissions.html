How to Apply

Four Steps to Engineering Excellence

We evaluate candidates holistically, emphasizing mathematical preparation, technical curiosity, and problem-solving passion.

1

Online Registration

Complete the prospective registration form with your intended engineering discipline, contact channels, and study mode preference.

2

Submit Credentials

Upload official high school or university transcripts, proof of prerequisite calculus/physics coursework, and optional portfolio projects.

3

Department Review

Our faculty admissions committee reviews your dossier. Select applicants may be invited for an informal technical interview.

4

Offer & Enrollment

Receive your official admission package, merit scholarship determination, and confirm your seat with the enrollment deposit.
